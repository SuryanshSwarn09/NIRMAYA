# IEEE Citation Guide & Reference Templates

## In-Text Citation Rules
- In-text references are enclosed in square brackets on the line of text, inside punctuation:
  - *"As demonstrated in [1]..."*
  - *"Previous studies [2], [3] have shown..."*
  - *"Several architectures [4]–[7] implement store-and-forward caching."*
- Never use *"Ref. [1]"* unless it starts a sentence: *"Reference [1] provides an exhaustive survey..."*

## Bibliography Templates

### 1. Journal Article
```text
[1] J. K. Author, "Name of paper," IEEE Trans. Software Eng., vol. 48, no. 4, pp. 1120-1135, Apr. 2022, doi: 10.1109/TSE.2021.3078901.
```

### 2. Conference Proceedings
```text
[2] S. Swarn and P. Sharma, "High-throughput sovereign health interoperability with Indian LGD governance," in Proc. IEEE Int. Conf. Healthc. Inform. (ICHI), Bengaluru, India, 2026, pp. 45-56.
```

### 3. National Standard / Government Specification
```text
[3] National Health Authority (NHA), "Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) Health Information Exchange & Consent Manager (HIE-CM) Technical Specifications v0.5," Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Govt. of India, Tech. Rep. ABDM-TS-05, Nov. 2023.
```

### 4. International Standard
```text
[4] Health Level Seven International, "HL7 FHIR Release 4: Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources," ANSI/HL7 Standard R4, Feb. 2020. [Online]. Available: https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/
```

### 5. Cryptographic / Security Specification
```text
[5] D. J. Bernstein, "Curve25519: New Diffie-Hellman speed records," in Public Key Cryptography - PKC 2006, M. Yung et al., Eds., Berlin, Germany: Springer, 2006, pp. 207-228.
```
