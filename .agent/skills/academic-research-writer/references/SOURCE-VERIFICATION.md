# Source Verification Protocol for Academic Rigor

## 1. Hierarchy of Credibility
1. **Tier 1 (Authoritative Standards & High-Impact Journals):**
   - IEEE Transactions, ACM Computing Surveys, Nature Medicine, The Lancet Digital Health, JMIR.
   - SDO specifications: ISO, HL7 International, W3C, IETF RFCs.
   - Government regulatory frameworks: National Health Authority (NHA) ABDM specs, Ministry of Law (DPDP Act 2023), UIDAI.
2. **Tier 2 (Premier Peer-Reviewed Conferences):**
   - IEEE ICHI, ACM SIGKDD, USENIX Security, ACM CCS, AMIA Annual Symposium.
3. **Tier 3 (Academic Books & Monographs):**
   - Published by MIT Press, Oxford University Press, Springer, Cambridge University Press, Wiley.
4. **Tier 4 (Vetted Preprints):**
   - arXiv / bioRxiv / medRxiv: Acceptable only for cutting-edge techniques if authored by recognized research institutions and verifiable via reproducible code/datasets.

## 2. Verification Protocol
- Verify DOI validity via `https://doi.org/<DOI>`.
- Cross-check citations on Google Scholar or OpenAlex to confirm publication history and academic community engagement.
- Ensure that technical claims regarding competitor architectures (e.g., OpenMRS, Bahmni, Epic) reference official technical documentation or published architectural evaluations.
